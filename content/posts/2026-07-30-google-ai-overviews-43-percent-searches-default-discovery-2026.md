Google's AI Overviews now appear on 43% of searches, according to [new data reported by TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/27/googles-ai-search-is-rapidly-becoming-the-default-new-data-shows/). The answer layer is no longer an experiment sitting above the blue links — it is where nearly half of all discovery now resolves. For brands, the question has moved from how to rank to whether they appear inside the answer at all.

## By the numbers

| Signal | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Searches showing an AI Overview | 43% | TechCrunch |
| U.S. searches ending without any click | 68.01% | SparkToro / Similarweb |
| Result click rate with vs without a summary | 8% vs 15% | Pew Research |
| Clicks on a source cited inside the summary | 1% | Pew Research |
| Web-mention vs backlink correlation with AI visibility | 0.664 vs 0.218 | Ahrefs |

Read together, these figures describe a single event: discovery is moving inside the answer, and the click that used to prove it is vanishing.

## Google now answers nearly half of every search before the first click

The 43% figure is not a slow creep. It is one of the fastest feature rollouts Google has ever shipped to Search, expanding from roughly 30% of queries to nearly half in a matter of months. Google has decided, at scale, that a generated answer belongs above the traditional results for a huge share of what people type.

This is not limited to informational trivia. Commercial and comparison queries — the kind a buyer runs before choosing a vendor — increasingly return an AI Overview first, with Gemini-powered AI Mode extending the same behavior into a conversational surface. The result page has become the destination, not the doorway.

## The clicks are not moving — they are disappearing

The traffic consequence is now well measured. In [SparkToro's June 2026 study with Similarweb](https://sparktoro.com/blog/in-2026-less-than-one-third-of-google-searches-still-send-a-click/), 68.01% of U.S. Google searches in the first four months of 2026 ended without a single click — not without a click to your site, without a click to anywhere.

SparkToro co-founder Rand Fishkin frames the arc bluntly: zero-click sat near 45% a decade ago, crossed 60% in 2024, and reached 68% in 2026 — the fastest two-year acceleration on record. Put in absolute terms, only 276 of every 1,000 searches now reach the open web, down from 374 two years earlier. Google is not losing from this shift. It is keeping users inside its own walls and monetizing the behavior directly.

## Inside the summary, almost nobody clicks the source

Even when a brand is cited in an AI Overview, the click rarely follows. The [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) tracked the browsing of 900 U.S. adults and found that when an AI summary appeared, users clicked a traditional result just 8% of the time, versus 15% when no summary was present — nearly half the click rate.

The link inside the summary did even worse: users clicked a cited source in only 1% of visits. And they were more likely to leave entirely, ending their session 26% of the time on pages with a summary compared with 16% without. Pew found 58% of respondents saw at least one AI Overview in a single month. The citation, in other words, is now the reward — not the click behind it.

## Ranking and being cited have become two different games

For twenty years, the discipline was singular: rank on page one and earn the click. That equation has split in two. A page can hold the top organic position and never appear in the AI Overview above it, while a source the model trusts gets synthesized into the answer that most users actually read.

This is why [AI visibility](https://machinerelations.ai/glossary/ai-visibility) is emerging as a distinct measurement problem from search rank. ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews each assemble answers from their own trust signals, and a brand can be strong in one and invisible in another. Tracking position in Google tells you almost nothing about whether an engine will name you when a buyer asks it a category question.

## What actually gets a brand into the answer

The most useful recent evidence on this comes from [Ahrefs' study of 75,000 brands](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/). It found that brand web mentions show the strongest correlation with AI Overview visibility (0.664 on the Spearman scale) — far stronger than backlinks (0.218). The top three factors were all off-site: web mentions, branded anchors, and branded search volume.

The spread is severe. Brands earning the most web mentions pulled up to 10x more AI Overview appearances than the next quartile, while 26% of brands studied had zero AI mentions at all. Correlation is not causation, as Ahrefs notes, but the direction is consistent with what every engine rewards: being talked about across trusted sources, structured through deliberate [citation architecture](https://machinerelations.ai/glossary/citation-architecture) so a model can extract and attribute you cleanly.

## FAQ

### Do AI Overviews really appear on 43% of searches?

Yes. TechCrunch reported the 43% figure in July 2026, up from roughly 30% months earlier. Pew Research separately found that 58% of tracked users saw at least one AI Overview within a single month, confirming the feature is now a default part of the Google results page, not an edge case.

### If my page ranks number one, will it be cited in the AI Overview?

Not necessarily. Ranking and citation are now separate outcomes. Ahrefs' 75,000-brand study found that off-site signals like brand web mentions (0.664 correlation) predicted AI Overview visibility far better than backlinks (0.218). A top organic result can be omitted from the answer while a more-mentioned source is synthesized into it.

### What is the difference between SEO and getting cited by AI engines?

SEO optimizes for rank and clicks on a list of links. Getting cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI Overviews depends on whether an engine trusts your brand enough to name it inside a generated answer. Machine Relations treats that citation, not the click, as the visibility outcome.

### How do I know if my brand appears in AI answers?

Track share of mention across engines rather than keyword rank. Query the questions your buyers actually ask in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode, and record how often your brand is named and cited. An AI visibility audit automates that baseline across multiple engines.
